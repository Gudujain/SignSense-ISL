# ISL content verification

## Status

All 35 bundled sign images and descriptions are **unverified**. Filenames and curriculum labels are inherited project data, not proof that a sign is represented correctly. Do not use this list as a teaching authority. Sign 9 is specifically flagged because its image may depict a thumbs-down.

## Review sources and process

- Numbers: compare against the ISLRTC “Numbers in ISL” material and its linked video: [ISLRTC resource page](https://islrtc.nic.in/documents/page/10/), [video](https://youtu.be/sVT6S-xJcbQ).
- Letters: compare against the ISLRTC “Alphabet in ISL” material and its linked video: [ISLRTC resource page](https://islrtc.nic.in/documents/page/10/), [video](https://youtu.be/VdwKSyza5oI).
- The ISLRTC dictionary includes regional variation. Ask a Deaf ISL educator or fluent Deaf signer to confirm each label, handshape, orientation, movement, and image pairing before changing a status to Verified. The project has not completed this review.

ISLRTC describes its dictionary as video entries with English/Hindi equivalents and notes regional variation. The project’s still images may not encode context or variants. See the [ISLRTC dictionary FAQ](https://islrtc.nic.in/faq/).

## Recording review

For each reviewed sign, record the reviewer name/role (with permission), date, regional variant, authoritative source entry, corrections to label/instruction/hand count, and explicit approval for the teaching use. Keep the original asset and review history. Do not mark signs verified from model predictions or learner reports.

See [`docs/hci/limitations.md`](hci/limitations.md), [`docs/hci/design-rationale.md`](hci/design-rationale.md), and [`docs/demo-script.md`](demo-script.md).
