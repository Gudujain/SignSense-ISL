    About and beta status

    SignSense stores progress locally. Camera frames go to this app on your computer for hand tracking. Frames are processed in memory and not saved.

    The 35 pose templates are experimental and need review by an ISL educator. They do not verify learning progress.

    How the sign check works: It looks at hand shapes and positions, then compares them with examples. It can make mistakes. Use the reference too. Feedback is not a verified grade.

    Credits: SignSense is adapted from the Indian Sign Language Recognition project by Karthikeyan et al.

    Practice tips

    1. 💡
      Use even light

      Face a window or lamp. Keep bright light behind the camera.

    2. 📏
      Find a comfortable distance

      Keep your hands large enough to see, with room to move.

    3. 🙌
      Keep each hand in view

      Use the guide to check that your hands fit inside the picture.

    4. 🖼️
      Look at the example

      Compare the hand shape and direction before you begin.

    5. 🔁
      Take your time

      You can retry, review the example or skip camera practice.
