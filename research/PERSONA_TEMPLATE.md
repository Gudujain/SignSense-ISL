# SignSense Evidence-Based Persona Template

> **Purpose**: Standardized template for synthesizing user groups and interview findings into actionable HCI personas. All personas constructed prior to final empirical interview completion must be explicitly labeled as **[Provisional Hypothesis]**.

---

## Persona Profile Structure

### 1. Identity & Overview
- **Name & Representative Title**: [e.g., Aanya — The Self-Paced Hearing Beginner]
- **Status**: [Provisional Hypothesis / Validated Empirical]
- **Age / Demographic Range**: [e.g., 22–30]
- **Occupation / Role**: [e.g., Undergraduate Student, Special Education Teacher, Sibling of Deaf Child]
- **Prior ISL Exposure**: [None / Basic / Intermediate / Fluent]

### 2. Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Learning Goal**: [What specific milestone are they trying to reach?]
- **Underlying Motivations**: [Why does learning ISL matter to them personally or professionally?]

### 3. Context of Use & Environment
- **Physical Environment**: [e.g., Home bedroom, quiet study hall, classroom, office]
- **Hardware & Device Constraints**: [e.g., Low-cost laptop, integrated 720p webcam, variable indoor lighting, no external microphone]

### 4. Primary Difficulties & Pain Points
- **Motor / Handshape Challenges**: [e.g., Difficult two-handed intersections, pinky independence]
- **Cognitive / Memory Frustrations**: [e.g., Forgetting signs after 3 days without practice]
- **Technological Frustrations**: [e.g., Inflexible AI systems that penalize valid attempts due to camera angle]

### 5. Accessibility & Interaction Requirements
- **Sensory & Visual Needs**: [e.g., High contrast, large clear buttons, zero reliance on audio feedback]
- **Pacing & Motor Control**: [e.g., Needs ample preparation time before recognition triggers]

### 6. Preferred Feedback Style
- **Desired Tone & Content**: [e.g., Constructive hints over cold error messages, transparent uncertainty flags]
- **Agency Expectations**: [e.g., Demands ability to override incorrect classifier predictions]

### 7. Representative Scenario
[A 2–3 paragraph realistic narrative illustrating a typical practice session, the friction encountered, and how SignSense resolves it.]

### 8. Concrete Design Implications for SignSense
- **Implication 1**: [Specific UI/UX requirement derived from this persona]
- **Implication 2**: [Adaptive engine requirement derived from this persona]
- **Implication 3**: [Feedback requirement derived from this persona]
