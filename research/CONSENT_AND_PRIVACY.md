# SignSense Informed Consent & Privacy Protocol

> **Important scope note:** This usability-study consent explicitly does not permit storing webcam stills. ML dataset collection requires the separate [Dataset Image Consent Addendum](DATASET_IMAGE_CONSENT_ADDENDUM.md); do not collect images under this form alone.

> **Document Code**: ETH-ISL-2026-CONSENT  
> **Study Title**: Human-Computer Interaction Evaluation of SignSense: An Adaptive Indian Sign Language Learning System  
> **Principal Investigator**: Antigravity Engineering & HCI Research Team

---

## 1. Study Purpose & Overview
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating the usability, accessibility, and pedagogical experience of **SignSense**, an interactive computer-vision assisted Indian Sign Language (ISL) learning prototype. The objective of this study is to investigate how real-time recognition feedback, learner-controlled error recovery, and explainable recommendations support novice and intermediate ISL learners.

---

## 2. Participant Activities
If you consent to participate, you will be asked to:
1. Complete a brief pre-study demographic and background questionnaire (5 minutes).
2. Perform a series of guided ISL practice tasks using a laptop/computer equipped with a standard webcam (15–20 minutes).
3. Complete a post-task usability questionnaire and participate in a short semi-structured interview (10–15 minutes).

---

## 3. Strict Camera & Video Privacy Guarantees
SignSense is built on a **local-first privacy architecture**:
- **Zero Video Uploads**: Video captured by your webcam is analyzed in real-time in your local browser and local machine.
- **No Video Recordings Stored**: No raw video footage or still webcam photographs are recorded, stored to persistent disk, or uploaded to external cloud services or third parties.
- **Local Data Only**: Practice metrics (e.g., attempt counts, response latencies, and confirmed correctness) are stored locally in your machine's private application database (`signsense.db`).
- **Research Analytics**: If you grant optional research consent, only anonymized performance numbers (latency, consensus ratios, and questionnaire responses) will be included in the academic report.

---

## 4. Risks, Benefits, and Voluntary Participation
- **Risks**: There are no foreseeable physical or psychological risks beyond normal computer usage and gentle arm/hand movement during sign practice.
- **Benefits**: You will gain hands-on introductory exposure to authentic Indian Sign Language fingerspelling and numerals.
- **Voluntary Participation**: Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question, take breaks, or withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
